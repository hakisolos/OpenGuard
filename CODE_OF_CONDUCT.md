# Code of Conduct

OpenGuard contributors are expected to keep discussions professional, respectful, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Keep reviews about the code, not the person.
- Respect maintainers' time by providing clear context.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discriminatory language, and personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Threats or harassment
- Discriminatory remarks
- Publishing private information without consent
- Deliberately derailing technical discussions
- Abusive behavior in issues, pull requests, or community spaces

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers when possible.
